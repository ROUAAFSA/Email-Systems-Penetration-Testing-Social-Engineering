About NexaCorp

Integrity, Innovation,
and Lasting Impact

Founded in 2005, NexaCorp Financial Services has grown from a boutique advisory firm into a full-service financial institution serving over 850 corporate clients and 3,000 individual investors.

Our multidisciplinary team of financial professionals, analysts, and strategists brings decades of combined experience across global markets, delivering solutions that are both innovative and grounded in fiduciary responsibility.
